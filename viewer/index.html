QUICK GUIDE

Getting around

Moving the camera

Drag
Look around. Click the scene first so it has focus.
Fullscreen
Use the top-right button for unlimited mouse-look without dragging. Press Esc to exit. Your pointer must be captured first.
Scroll wheel
Move forward or backward in the direction you are looking.
W A S D
Walk forward, left, backward and right.
Space / Shift
Fly up / down.
Q / E
Hold to orbit left / right around the flyer’s middle while keeping your viewing direction relative to it.
Double-W / F
Start sprinting at 1.5× player speed. Sprint ends when you stop moving.
Click a block
Inspect it: its state, owner and coordinates appear in the right panel. Click empty space to deselect.

Movement keys only work while the scene is focused. Clicking anywhere else, or opening a popup, releases them.

Playback

▶ / Ⅱ
Play or pause.

Step once. The Left and Right arrow keys step backward and forward too.
→ / ←
Switch between playing forward and backward.
↺
Reset to the starting state.
Game speed
0.1× to 30×. At 1× the simulation runs 10 ticks per second.

Ticks or Detailed

The switch at the bottom of the right panel picks how finely you step. Ticks advances a whole game tick at a time. Detailed breaks one tick into its individual power and piston actions, adds a label with the current action to the scene, and lets you show or hide power paths, move discovery, moving owners and chunk boundaries.

Flyers and tools

Browse flyer bank
Search, filter and sort the collection, or click a bar in the chart to open a fastest flyer at that push limit. Flyers open paused.
Open .flyer
Load a file from your computer. It stays in your browser and is never uploaded.
Show floor
Toggle the fading ground grid in the right panel.
Follow flyer
Smoothly move with the average position of its blocks; manual movement still works.
Motion display
Real shows moving blocks in their stored destination cells and keeps the familiar midpoint piston head. Halfway places moving blocks at their midpoint too. Smooth animates blocks and piston heads; manual steps settle halfway, while continuous playback completes the move. These choices are visual only.
