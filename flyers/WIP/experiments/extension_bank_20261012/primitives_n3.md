## Notes
- ext0 (empty extension) events counted but not recorded: 138 total over 16 flyers.
- Every N3 flyer is 5-phase cyclic (words are rotations of mmmww / mwwmm...); the rotation canonicalisation therefore collapses almost everything to target mmmww (plain 5-body designs) or mmwmw (pl14/pl15/pl17 designs).
- Only 17 keys occur in >=2 flyers; the rest are multi-carrier pulls (carrier lists include bodies touching the actor) or one-offs. Lowest loads: push with no carrier (5-7), pull from mwwmm (8).
- Window stability (W0=100 vs 150): firing order of interchangeable pistons varies; 1-3 keys per flyer appear in only one window; the union is kept (records with "window":150).
- pl102 loads reach ~100 (mass-pulls of 100+ block bodies); bodies there are partial pulls (e.g. B0x45 of 141), so target n is not a full body. Treat pl102 loads as upper bounds of a different regime.
- pl106/pl126/pl127 pulling3_tenbody are PL variants of pl102 (not processed).
- No parse failures; no target fallbacks used. mmwmw keys: pl14 mmwmw_two_pulls, pl15 exclusive_roles, pl17, pl34 (loads 13+).
