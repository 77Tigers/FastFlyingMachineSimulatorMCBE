## Added front relay architecture and screen

`extra_front.py` is a runnable generator for four roles per group: back T, middle M, front F, and an **extra front relay A**. There are 20 independently represented sticky carriers. A shares T’s three-advance phase and sits near the next group’s T. The two +X pistons that push the next T are assigned to A instead of the previous T, reducing generated back sticky segments to five cells. A’s own +X drive sites are assigned to the next group’s nearby M; F retains the existing sticky-pull control. The generator routes prospective contacts through all five phase geometries and rejects fixed collisions, same-material premature adhesion, and connections beyond role caps. A generated layout is only a geometric candidate; the Rust simulator determines whether it moves.

An early version gave A only **one** drive piston. Its nominal three-advance schedule was therefore impossible. It generated 54 layouts from 162 seeds across two lateral configurations. `relay_results.tsv` records Rust 160-tick tests at diagnostic PL100: **all 54 had distance zero**, despite local piston extensions. The representative `relay_screen/c0s14.flyer` and `c0s14_first.txt` preserve the failure. An isolated retracted piston at serialized `(14,0,16)` pins the minimum X; a hand-added two-slime bridge moved the file only one block in 160 ticks. These 54 results are exploratory failures, not a valid screen of the corrected schedule or PL17.

I repaired A to have three +X drive sites and seeded sticky contact to otherwise orphaned piston hardware before routing. A bounded 50-seed test on centers `[(0,0),(0,8),(8,8),(8,0),(4,-6)]`, fronts `[0,1,1,1,2]`, caps `(8,17,11,11)` produced **no fully routed candidates**: 19 `route_unreachable_role1`, 13 `route_unreachable_role3`, 9 `mandatory_collision`, 8 `distance_cap_role1`, 1 `mandatory_adhesion`. No Rust motion test or speed claim applies to this repaired family yet. The front relay's three pistons shift a substantial routing burden onto M, which now needs a better local geometry. No layout from this experiment reached a PL17 3 bps audit, 10,000 ticks, phase sample, or conservation proof; no result was banked.
