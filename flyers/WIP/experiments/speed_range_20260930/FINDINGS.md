## User architecture guidance

Helpers go directly ahead of what they pull. Power may come from the segment
ahead of the helper. Add a helper-helper ahead: it powers the helper sticky
and pulls the helper one block, removing that back-chain push. The helper
still receives two back pushes and pushes the helper-helper; an additional
extension ahead may supply power. Combine this with compact side pickup.
Avoid slime/honey behind a piston being transported. Do not push two segments
together for this task: the user says that is usually too expensive at these
speeds and explicitly excludes the2.5bps exception.

**Further clarification:** start from PL18, convert one segment first (possibly
the one after the highest-load segment), then the rest. The added pair are
**two forward normal pistons sharing a redstone block**, not two new sticky
pistons. Only add at most two piston passengers to an existing segment; do
not grow that segment by a six-cell power connection. Helpers are separate
bodies, so their own glue and sources are counted in their own movements.

`separate_helpers.py` now uses existing side contacts on original body3 to
carry the two added normals, with no added original-body glue. New helper0
is driven twice by those normals, pulls old body3 on its last move, and is
pulled by new helper2. Helper2 supplies its power and initially uses a
three-normal group. Existing body4 supplies helper2's sticky power. The old
body3 last normal is removed. This is a proposed seven-body assembly, not a
verified flyer. Initial18,000 fixed-layout placements with alternating helper
materials and18,000 with both helpers honey produce no routed candidate;
they test ascending-coordinate anchors, not all possible placements. A
forward-leaf-first anchor order is a distinct bounded follow-up.

Follow-ups completed:18,000 forward-leaf-first cases with both helpers honey
and16,800 cases using original body0, the segment after original body3 in the
back-drive chain. Neither produced a routed candidate. The user explicitly
approved the helper-helper's temporary three-normal drive. The reusable
[timing contract](FRONT_HELPER_CONTRACT.md) gives exact initial reach,
representative passenger trajectories and the still-unresolved interfaces.
Stop these fixed-original-geometry placement families; repeating their seeds
does not address the overlaps.

Two contract corrections matter for reuse: a -X pull starts at base-2, so the
helper0 sticky pulling old body3 initially sits **contact+1**, not contact-1;
and the three-normal helper2 group shares source power with interchangeable
ready members. Forbidding power outside each observed member's nominal firing
slot wrongly rejects valid group eligibility. Both corrections are in the
current generator. The rejected placements are not an architectural lower
bound.
