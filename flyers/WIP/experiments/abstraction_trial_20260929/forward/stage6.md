# Stage 6 — assembly and routing (FAIL for bounded speculative assembly)

I placed four distinct action lanes at Z=0,2,4,6. A is a five-slime rail at X=0, Z=0..4; B is a five-honey rail at X=2, Z=2..6. Pistons start at A0 `(2,0,0)` extended, B0 `(4,0,2)` retracted, A1 `(3,0,4)` retracted, B1 `(5,0,6)` retracted. A single observer/stone pair powers B0's t0 reset. This is a labelled speculative routing attempt in `speculative_assembly.flyer`, generated by `motif_probe.py`. It is format-valid and uses push limit 10, but does not satisfy the inherited full-cycle contract.

The earliest known contract violation is at t0. Stage 2 says A1 and B1 ride A at the cycle-boundary A pull (its "next t0" wraps around). The assembly initialized them at their later reset/action coordinates, X=3 and X=5, instead of deriving initial locations and support from their transport history. A0's t0 pull moved only the five A rail blocks; neither piston rode A. After one tick A1 remained at `(3,0,4)` and B1 at `(5,0,6)`. Their later numeric X coordinates therefore appear plausible by coincidence, but their transport histories are wrong. This is my implementation/interpretation error, not a deficiency in the supplied handoff.

At t2 B0 then pulled six blocks: five B rail blocks plus A1, which was face-adjacent at `(3,0,4)`. A1 reached `(4,0,4)` by t4, violating stage 3's required pull base X=3. No later power pulses were implemented, so t2 A1 reset and t4/t6 actuation also fail. There were no actions from t4 through t7. These are subsequent failures of the same bounded routing/pulse attempt, not evidence that the abstract mechanism is impossible or that coordinates should have been supplied.

First composition check: material rails are connected within themselves, the starting file passes editor `validate()`, and the simulator accepts it. But the route must carry A1 and B1 with A at t0, then meet their later transfers without premature pickup. A complete route also needs timed observer power and absence windows, support attachments for each piston, and actual per-action loads. Stage 2 supplies coarse ride slots; selecting exact pickup faces and transition geometry is expected stage-5/6 work, though a stronger handoff could make this interface easier to verify. I made one route choice and did not explore a broader family. See `screen.txt` for commands and raw output.
